BrightSprout Learning privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Sharp Top Barbershop LLC

In short
  • BrightSprout does not collect, sell or share personal information about your child.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.
  • What your child does in the app stays on your device.
  • The app downloads new stories and lessons without sending anything about your child.
  • Grown-ups can choose to send us feedback; it is never required.
  • Microphone practice is optional, off by default, and never records.

What the app stores, and where

When you set up the app, you enter a child's first name or nickname, age and avatar. As your child plays, the app saves their progress: lessons finished, stars, skill progress, time spent and settings. All of this is stored only on your device. The app does not send it to us or to anyone else.

To delete it, open Settings (behind the parent gate) and delete the child's profile, or choose "Erase everything", or delete the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

The Spanish world has optional speaking practice. It is off until a grown-up turns it on in the Grown-ups area, behind the parent gate. iOS then asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition.

  • Speech is recognized on the device by Apple's built-in speech recognizer. The app only offers the microphone when your device can recognize Spanish without the internet, and it always asks for on-device recognition, so your child's voice is not sent to a server.
  • Nothing is recorded or kept. The app only checks whether it heard the phrase, to say "¡Te escuché!" (I heard you!), and then discards the result.
  • Speaking is never required. Every speaking step can be done by tapping. You can turn microphone practice off, or remove the permission in iOS Settings, at any time.

New stories and lessons

When the device is online, the app checks for new or updated stories, lessons, words and building challenges, and downloads them. The request carries only the app's public key: no name, age, progress, account or device identifier. Like any download, it reveals the device's network address to our content service (Supabase), which keeps it in standard server logs. Downloaded content is stored on the device so it works offline.

Parent feedback

Behind the parent gate, a grown-up can send us feedback: a category, a message, an optional star rating, an optional game it is about, an optional email address for a reply, and an optional screenshot chosen from the photo library. The app adds its version, the platform (iPhone or iPad) and the iOS version. It never adds the child's name, age, progress or anything the child said or made.

Feedback is stored by our service provider, Supabase, is readable only by us, and is deleted after 12 months, or sooner if you ask by email. To limit spam, the service keeps a one-way, salted daily fingerprint of the network address for 24 hours; it never stores the address itself. The app asks for photo access only when you choose to attach a screenshot, and you can choose a single picture instead of granting access to all photos.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Supabase hosts our content downloads and parent feedback as our service provider and uses the data only to provide that service. Playing a lesson does not contact any outside service; new content is downloaded in the background as described above. Apple provides the operating system, the App Store and TestFlight; their handling of data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

BrightSprout is made for children ages 3 to 8. We do not collect personal information from children. Feedback can only be sent by a grown-up, behind the parent gate. The app has no chat, no social features and no links out to the web for children.

TestFlight beta testing

If you test BrightSprout through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports, usage information and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's TestFlight terms. Testers who join through a public link do not share their email address with us. If you send feedback through TestFlight, we see what you write and any screenshot you attach, and use it only to improve the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
