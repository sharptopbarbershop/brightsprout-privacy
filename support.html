Get help

Email sharptopbarbershop@gmail.com and we'll reply within two business days. You can also send feedback from inside the app: open the Grown-ups area (behind the parent gate), then Feedback.

Common questions

Does BrightSprout need the internet? No. Every lesson, story and voice is built in. When online, the app downloads new stories and lessons in the background.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts.

How do I reset or remove a child's progress? Grown-ups area → Settings → delete the child's profile, or "Erase everything".

Spanish speaking practice doesn't hear my child. It is optional and off by default. Turn it on in the Grown-ups area; it needs iOS on-device Spanish recognition. Every speaking step can also be done by tapping.

Ages: BrightSprout is designed for children ages 3 to 8. Lessons adapt to the age you choose and to how your child is doing.
